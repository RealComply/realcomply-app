1. Who we are

RealComply Pty Ltd ("RealComply", "we", "us", "our") operates realcomply.com.au and the RealComply compliance platform, a software-as-a-service product that helps NSW real estate agents and agencies prepare, evidence and maintain their regulatory compliance. RealComply is an APP entity under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and handles personal information in accordance with the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs).

2. Scope of this policy

This policy covers personal information we handle across three contexts:

  • Visitors to our website — anyone browsing realcomply.com.au or contacting us through it.
  • Our customers — the agencies and individual agents who sign up for and use the RealComply platform, and the people at those agencies who hold user accounts.
  • Our own business operations — including our use of third-party business tools (such as Meta's advertising and Business Manager tools) to market and run RealComply as a company.

Where RealComply processes personal information on behalf of a customer agency (see section 4), that agency is the entity primarily responsible to the individuals concerned; RealComply acts as a processor/custodian of that data, not its owner.

4. Information our customers upload (agency & client data)

To provide the compliance service, agencies using RealComply upload material that can include personal and financial information about their own clients — for example vendor and purchaser identity documents, contracts of sale, agency agreements, trust account records, and complaint or gift records.

For this category of information:

  • RealComply holds it as a custodian on the agency's behalf, to provide the compliance platform — not as the owner of that data.
  • We do not use it for any secondary purpose, sell it, or use it to train external AI models.
  • Access is restricted by role (licensee-in-charge vs agent) and by tenant isolation — one agency can never see another agency's data.
  • Sensitive fields (identity documents, trust data) receive a higher level of protection and are masked in the product interface where practical.
  • If you are a client of one of our customer agencies and have a question about how your information is held, your agency is the right first point of contact, as they control the purpose and content of what's uploaded; RealComply will also assist directly on request.

5. How we use information

We use personal information only for the purpose it was collected, or a purpose you'd reasonably expect, including to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the RealComply platform for our customers;
  • Create and administer user accounts and manage access permissions;
  • Respond to enquiries and provide customer support;
  • Send service communications (e.g. compliance review reminders, account or billing notices);
  • With your consent or on an opt-out basis where permitted, send marketing communications about RealComply;
  • Improve, secure, and troubleshoot our website and platform;
  • Meet our own legal, accounting and regulatory obligations.

6. Who we share information with

We don't sell personal information. We share it only with the following categories of recipients, each engaged to help us run RealComply:

  • Hosting & infrastructure — our application and database hosting providers, used to run the platform securely.
  • AI processing — our AI model provider, used to help analyse and summarise compliance documents you or your agency upload as part of the service. Document text may be sent to this provider to generate the AI's output; it is not used by that provider to train their models under our commercial terms.
  • Email & communications tools — providers we use to send account, support and marketing emails.
  • Payment processing — a payment processor to handle subscription billing; we do not store full card details ourselves.
  • Advertising & analytics platforms — such as Meta and Google, used to run and measure our own marketing (see section 8).
  • Professional advisers & regulators — where required by law, or to obtain legal, accounting or insurance advice.
  • A buyer of all or part of RealComply's business, should that occur, subject to the buyer agreeing to handle information consistently with this policy.

We maintain a list of these subprocessors and review each for its security practices; a current list is available on request.

7. Overseas disclosure

We aim to keep customer platform data hosted in Australia. Some of the third-party providers listed in section 6 — for example our AI model provider, or global advertising platforms — may store or process information on servers located outside Australia. Where this occurs, we take reasonable steps to satisfy ourselves that the overseas recipient handles personal information consistently with the Australian Privacy Principles, including through the provider's own contractual and security commitments.

8. Advertising, cookies & Meta / Google tools

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies, and business tools provided by platforms such as Meta (Facebook/Instagram) and Google, for two purposes:

8.1 Running our own advertising

RealComply uses Meta's advertising products (including Meta Business Manager, Ads Manager, and where applicable the Meta Marketing API) to plan, run and measure our own ad campaigns promoting RealComply. This use is limited to managing our own advertising account and reviewing aggregated performance reporting (e.g. impressions, clicks, conversions) — we do not use these tools to collect personal information about individual Facebook or Instagram users beyond what those platforms themselves provide as standard, aggregated ad-reporting data.

8.2 Measuring our website and ads

We may use cookies and pixels (such as a Meta Pixel or Google Analytics/Ads tag) on our website to understand how visitors use our site and how our ads perform. These tools may set cookies on your device and share limited data (such as pages viewed or that an ad led to a visit) with Meta or Google in line with their own privacy policies.

You can control cookies through your browser settings, and manage how Meta and Google use your data for advertising through Meta's Ad Preferences and Google's Ad Settings.

9. Security

We protect personal information with:

  • Encryption in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest for stored files and database fields;
  • Access controls, including tenant isolation between customer agencies and role-based access within each agency;
  • Authentication safeguards, including multi-factor authentication for the most sensitive account role;
  • Audit logging of access to and export of sensitive records; and
  • Hosting with reputable providers with strong security practices, with an Australian data region used wherever practical.

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps proportionate to the sensitivity of the information involved.

10. Retention & deletion

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purpose it was collected, including to meet record-keeping obligations relevant to real estate compliance (which can require multi-year retention). Where practical, we prefer to retain an attestation or outcome record (e.g. "identity verified, method X, date") rather than a full copy of an identity document, and apply shorter retention with stronger protection where a copy must be kept. When a customer agency's account ends, we provide an export of their data and delete or return it on the timeline set out in our customer agreement.

11. Access, correction & complaints

You can ask us for access to the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to correct it, by contacting us using the details in section 15. We'll respond within a reasonable time and may need to verify your identity first. If your information was uploaded by an agency you deal with as their client, we may need to involve that agency to action your request.

If you're not satisfied with how we've handled your personal information, you can contact us directly, or lodge a complaint with the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at oaic.gov.au.

12. Data breach notification

We maintain an incident response process to detect, contain, assess and respond to any data breach. Where a breach is likely to result in serious harm, we will notify the OAIC and affected individuals in line with the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme, and will tell any affected customer agency promptly so they can manage their own obligations to their clients.

13. Children's information

RealComply is a business-to-business product for licensed real estate agents and agencies. Our website and platform are not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time as our business, products or legal obligations change. The "last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent version. Material changes will be highlighted on our website.
